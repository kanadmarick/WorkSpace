# Glossary

## AI Backend Engineer

An engineer who blends backend systems design, distributed systems, API contracts, operational reliability, and AI/model-serving constraints into production-grade systems.

## ADR

Architecture Decision Record. A concise document describing a decision, context, trade-offs, and consequences.

## Aether

The model- and inference-oriented component of the portfolio, focusing on local inference and service boundaries.

## Evidence

A reviewable artifact that demonstrates a real outcome: benchmark result, technical doc, deployed feature, or demo.

## Hadron

A portfolio concept representing internal model infrastructure or agent orchestration concerns, often connected to execution and control logic.

## Inference gateway

A boundary that routes requests to models while enforcing timeouts, fallbacks, versioning, and usage limits.

## Memory

The operational state of the project: progress, evidence, and the story behind the work.

## Portfolio ecosystem

The set of project concepts designed to support a coherent senior AI backend narrative: Sentinel, Hadron/Aether, and Zenith.

## Sentinel

The safety and remediation-focused component intended to demonstrate controlled agentic operations with observability and policy constraints.

## Zenith

The analytics or market-facing component designed to demonstrate useful end-user value and resilience under failure conditions.

## Roadmap phase

A defined period of work with a measurable outcome and expected deliverables.

## SLO

Service Level Objective. A measurable target for reliability or latency that evaluates whether the system meets operational expectations.

## Threat model

A structured analysis of risks, attack surfaces, and mitigations relevant to a system or component.

## Workflow graph

A conceptual map of tasks, dependencies, and decisions that connects execution to outcomes.
